OpenUPI

A native voice concierge for Indian commerce and payments, with a careful human approval step.

PROJECT STATUS

Private · testing

BUILT WITH
SwiftUIVoice AIUPIiOS
Discuss this project ↗

OpenUPI is a native iOS voice concierge for Indian digital commerce and payments. It brings a bilingual Hindi and English interface together with grocery discovery, QR scanning, and a path to legacy UPI rails.

One conversation, several surfaces

The prototype connects a voice-first interaction to focused interfaces: a Blinkit grocery engine, a UPI QR scanner, and a hardware bridge for GSM and USSD experiments. Fabits wealth flows are part of the same exploration.

The constraint

Money-moving actions need a clear verbal confirmation. The assistant can prepare a flow, but it does not silently approve a payment on someone’s behalf.
