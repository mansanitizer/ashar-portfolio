Duomaxxing

A paired habit and challenge app built around check-ins, milestones, and shared momentum.

PROJECT STATUS

Android + iOS · testing

BUILT WITH
AndroidiOSSocial habits
Discuss this project ↗

Duomaxxing is a paired challenge app for people who want a little more structure and a little more accountability. It is in testing on Android and iOS.

Shared progress

People set criteria, check in, and see progress across daily and monthly views. Feeds, milestones, locations, photos, notifications, and small companion characters make the routine feel social without turning it into a leaderboard.

What is still changing

The testing phase is focused on the rhythm of a check-in: what should be visible, what should stay private, and how a pair can recover after missing a day.
