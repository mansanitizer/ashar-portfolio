01 / PRODUCT STRATEGY & USER PROBLEM

Solving Investment Decision Paralysis

company pivot & user research

During our strategic pivot towards mutual funds, I identified a core user problem through interviews and support tickets: users didn't know what to buy, when to sell, or which funds to pick for their goals.

Working as the solo PM with 2 engineers and 1 designer, I chose a basket-based approach to allow users to invest in diversified portfolios that could be adjusted over time based on specific goals like retirement, house purchase, or emergency funds.

  • Goal-Based Design: Simple goal selection to improve user confidence in investment decisions
  • Diversification Made Easy: Pre-built baskets to allow users to avoid single-fund risk
  • Admin Flexibility: Manual basket creation to allow admins to adjust allocations based on market conditions
02 / IMPLEMENTATION & TEAM COLLABORATION

Building with Limited Resources

product execution

Led end-to-end product development including user research, requirement documentation, wireframing, and stakeholder coordination. Given our small team size, I had to balance feature scope with technical bandwidth.

  • Technical Constraints: Built simple admin interface to allow manual basket creation due to limited engineering resources
  • User Experience: Collaborated with our designer to create intuitive goal selection flow to improve conversion rates
  • Strategic Planning: Prioritized core functionality over advanced features to allow faster time-to-market during company pivot

teamwork & results

Coordinated closely with engineering and design teams to ensure smooth execution despite resource constraints:

  • Engineering Collaboration: Worked with 2-person dev team to balance feature complexity with delivery timelines
  • Design Partnership: Collaborated on user flows and wireframes to improve user experience within design bandwidth
  • Stakeholder Management: Regular updates to leadership on progress and pivot strategy alignment
  • User Impact: Successfully launched goal-based investment solution that improved user decision-making confidence
